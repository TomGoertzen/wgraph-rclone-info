WGraph rclone

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026.

This policy describes WGraph rclone, a personal research integration operated by Tom Goertzen. It is not a service offered to the general public.

Data accessed and its use

With the account holder's authorization, rclone accesses Google Drive file contents and metadata, including filenames, folder identifiers, sizes, and modification times. The configured research workflow uses this access to retrieve existing W-graphs and to save computed graphs, checkpoints, figures, and statistics.

The requested Google Drive scope permits broad read and write access, including file deletion. It is not a folder-level security restriction. The workflow is configured to operate on designated research folders; it does not intentionally process unrelated personal files.

Storage and security

Research files are stored in the operator's Google Drive and may be processed in memory or stored on the operator's computers and research server. Operational logs may contain research filenames, transfer details, timings, and errors.

OAuth access and refresh tokens are kept in private rclone configuration files on those machines, with access restricted by filesystem permissions. They are not included in this public website or its repository. The application does not receive or store the Google account password.

Sharing

Data is transferred to Google Drive and the operator's configured computers and research server to perform the requested workflow. Google and the server provider process data as part of providing those services. This integration does not sell Google account data, use it for advertising, or automatically publish Drive contents through this website. The operator may separately choose to publish mathematical research results.

Retention, deletion, and revocation

Research files, checkpoints, logs, and local copies are retained until the operator removes them; there is no automatic deletion deadline. Files and copies can be deleted from Google Drive and the relevant machines, subject to those services' trash and backup retention.

Access can be revoked through Google Account connections. Revocation prevents further authorized API access but does not delete files or copies already stored. Local authorization data can also be removed from the rclone configuration.

This information website

These static pages contain no sign-in form, analytics scripts, or application-set cookies. They do not access visitors' Google Drive accounts. GitHub Pages hosts the public pages and may process ordinary website request data under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Contact

Contact Tom Goertzen using the support email shown on the Google authorization screen for questions about this integration or its data handling. Do not send passwords, client secrets, or OAuth tokens.
